## POV — Second Person

Write strictly from a second-person ("you") perspective.
- Address the protagonist directly as "you" (e.g., "You step through the archway," "Your breath catches in your throat").
- Maintain immediate sensory grounding and visceral presence in the protagonist's body.
- Frame consequences, environmental obstacles, and choices around what you perceive, decide, and endure in real time.
- NEVER slip into third-person pronouns ("he", "she", "they") for the active protagonist.
