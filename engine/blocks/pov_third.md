## POV — Close Third

Write strictly from a close third-person perspective.
- The narrator is restricted to the thoughts, feelings, and sensory perceptions of a single character.
- Use third-person pronouns only (he/him, she/her, they/them).
- NEVER use first-person pronouns ("I", "me", "my", "we", "our") in narrative prose outside of quoted dialogue.
- Avoid omniscient or distant framing. The reader should feel situated inside that character's experience in real time.
